# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 30, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
When you connect to this Minecraft server or interact with the associated Discord application, the following limited information may be collected:
* **Discord Data:** Discord User ID, username, and commands issued to or processed by the Discord application.
* **Minecraft Account & Technical Data:** Minecraft username, Universally Unique Identifier (UUID), IP address, connection logs, in-game chat logs, and latency metrics.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected information is used strictly for operating and maintaining the service:
* Providing access to the Minecraft server and associated Discord features.
* Moderating server interactions, maintaining community standards, and enforcing the Terms of Service.
* Troubleshooting server bugs, monitoring system performance, and defending against unauthorized access or malicious activity.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention
* Data is stored securely on the underlying system infrastructure (managed declaratively via NixOS).
* Server logs, player activity history, and plugin metrics are retained only as long as required for server operation, administration, and security.

## 4. Third-Party Data Processing
* We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
* Certain data is processed through essential third-party APIs required for core functionality, including **Discord** (for bot interactions) and **Mojang AB / Microsoft** (for Minecraft account authentication).

## 5. Data Security
We implement standard technical safeguards to protect your data against loss, misuse, or unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission over the internet or digital storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. Your Rights & Data Removal
If you wish to request the removal or deletion of your stored user data (such as plugin statistics or linked account logs), please contact the server administrator directly.

## 7. Policy Updates
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any revisions will be reflected in this file with an updated effective date.
